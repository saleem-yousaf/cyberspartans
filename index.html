Live

BreachForge

Our breach and attack simulation platform, and a CyberSpartans product. It runs safe, automated breach scenarios mapped to MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise, covering the full chain from initial access to command and control, and shows whether an attacker can reach what matters in your estate. It is the standard we hold commercial BAS tools to.

It also carries a built-in threat intelligence portal. Open source feeds from CISA KEV, MITRE ATT&CK, AlienVault OTX and abuse.ch, aggregated, mapped to ATT&CK and updated automatically, so you can see what is being exploited in the wild and how it maps to your environment.

Now live at breachforge.co.uk, behind a secure login. For access, contact info@cyberspartans.co.uk.
